# MJL Design Memory

Business behavior, roles, permissions, states, data, and scope come from the
canonical v2 package. The approved v3 package remains the presentation
authority only.

## Durable Direction

- Institutional, sober, calm, clear, trustworthy, and French-first.
- Hide unnecessary ERP complexity while revealing MJL work and status.
- Use v3 tokens, components, interaction states, density, responsive behavior,
  and accessibility guidance.
- Preserve direct route/POST authorization, active entity filtering, explicit
  status and locked states, contextual evidence seams, concise feedback, and
  meaningful non-color cues.
- Support required operational PDF/XLSX and supplemental audited CSV without
  making browser presentation a dependency of generated outputs.

## Product Boundary

The v3 package does not define target roles, Partner scopes, finance objects,
workflow transitions, document rules, or output formats. Where its product
text conflicts with canonical v2, v2 controls.
