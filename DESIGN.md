# MJL Design Memory

MJL product decisions come from `docs/mjl-authoritative-decisions.md`. The
approved v3 package under `docs/design-system/approved/v3/` is the durable
presentation authority within those business, security, permission, and
workflow boundaries.

## Durable direction

- Institutional, sober, calm, clear, trustworthy, and French-first.
- Hide unnecessary ERP complexity while revealing MJL work and status.
- Use the canonical v3 tokens, components, interaction states, density rules,
  responsive behavior, and accessibility rules.
- Preserve guarded routes, contextual documents, server-side scope controls,
  status-first workflows, actionable alerts, and first-class CSV/XLSX outputs.

conformance, client wording approval, and operator approval are not established
by this memory.
